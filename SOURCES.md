## Ongoing integrity checks

Run `python3 spamalot.py check-sources --suggest` to record the current published URLs and propose manual repairs. The checker does not assert acceptance or delivery of mail. After two consecutive clearly missing-page checks, the source becomes `broken` and is not included in `subscribe --all`. `export-defaults` creates a reduced source file without broken or disabled entries; neither operation deletes the historic source or its opt-out records.

OpenAI, Gemini and Perplexity searches can propose more sources via `discover`; the AI response is **never** verification and cannot silently populate the default source list. Read the individual publisher’s current postal opt-out procedure yourself.
