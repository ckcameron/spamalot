## Paper fuel warning

Physical paper is not inherently safe to burn. The U.S. EPA cautions against burning magazines with colored inks, cardboard, and household refuse in residential wood-burning appliances. Consult [EPA Burn Wise](https://www.epa.gov/burnwise/best-wood-burning-practices).
